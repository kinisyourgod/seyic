Our history

Six people in 2008. Over 200 today.

Brick installationWelding & fabricationMaintenance & repairsShotcreting

Seyic Refractory began on 15 June 2008 with six dedicated staff and a clear aim: reliable, high-quality refractory solutions. Through a commitment to excellence, innovation and constant improvement, we have grown into a trusted name known for timely service and solutions tailored to each client.

Long-term success, we believe, is built on strong relationships, integrity and professionalism.

Today more than 200 people work with the same passion that shaped our early days, protecting our clients, materials, tools and team as we aim for zero accidents during and after every project.
